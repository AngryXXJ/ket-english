## Part 1 · 短信息（Q1–6，p2–3 选录）

**4. 邮件 George → Peter**
> Hi Peter, I'm going to the history museum in the centre this afternoon. I want to see the new ancient Greek exhibit. Do you want to come round after school and then we can go together? Let me know, George
> 题：If he wants to go to the museum, Peter should …（A go to George's house / B meet George after class at school / C choose a place to meet George in the centre）

**5. 广告 Bingham's Shoes — Autumn Sale**
> Discounts in our store. Today only: 20% off all snow boots; half off all summer shoes. 40% off EVERYTHING in our online shop!
> 题：（A only for online customers / B for one day / C only includes summer shoes）

**6. 短信 Laura → Katie**
> Katie, I'm sorry I wasn't in Science class today. I'm not feeling well and I had to stay home. I spoke to Mr Gill — he said we can do our presentation on Monday. See you then! Laura
> 题：（A Katie stayed home from school today / B Mr Gill gave a test in Science class today / C Laura and Katie are doing a presentation together）

## Part 2 · My House（Q7–13，p4–5）

**Maria**: My family and I live in a small city in Arizona, USA. It's me, my parents and my older brother Jeremy. Our house isn't that big, but it's nice. My brother and I each have our own bedroom. We each have a desk there where we do our homework. We also have a living room, a kitchen and a dining room. We don't eat there very often, but my brother likes to play video games there and I play table tennis with my friends. It's lots of fun!

**Jennifer**: I live with my family on a farm in the countryside in Australia. We have horses and cows, and we grow corn in our fields. Our house is very big and old with lots of rooms. That's a good thing, because I have a big family — four brothers and one sister! So the bathroom is very busy in the morning before school! My brothers each have their own bedroom, but my sister and I are in the same one. We also have a study room where I can do my homework and there's a big shed behind the house where the animals stay. I love spending time in the shed with the animals, especially my horse, Snow!

**Mira**: I love the city life! I live in a flat in London with my mum, my dad and our dog, Max. Our flat isn't big, but it has enough room for us. We have a living room, a kitchen, a dining room, two bedrooms and two bathrooms — one is just for me! We live in the city centre, so it can be noisy at times. That's difficult when I'm trying to study. But I love having so much to do in my neighbourhood! There's a basketball court down the road where I play most days, as well as lots of shops, restaurants and cafés.

问题：7 Who does schoolwork in a special room? 8 Who likes to play a sport at home? 9 Who has her own bathroom? 10 Who has an extra building outside their house? 11 Who doesn't have an animal? 12 Who doesn't have any brothers or sisters? 13 Who has to share their bedroom?（A=Maria B=Jennifer C=Mira）

## Part 3 · An unusual visitor（Q14–18，p6）

> by Tom Smith
> A young emperor penguin from the Antarctic arrived on a beach in New Zealand yesterday. It's the first time people have seen an emperor penguin in New Zealand in 44 years.
> A woman who was walking her dog on the beach saw something white on the sand. At first, she thought it was a child's toy, but when she got closer, she saw the young penguin standing there on the beach. She couldn't believe her eyes!
> Experts say it's quite unusual to see a penguin in New Zealand. They believe that the young penguin got lost while hunting for fish and is now thousands of miles from home.
> This strange visitor has brought many curious people to the beach. Everyone is asked not to upset the penguin in any way and to keep their dogs on leads. The penguin is about 10 months old and 80 cm tall. Emperor penguins are the tallest and largest of the penguin species. They grow up to 122 cm tall and weigh more than 40 kg.

答案：14 B · 15 B · 16 C · 17 C · 18 B
